# Privacy Policy for Red Pentecostés Online **Effective date: August 24, 2026** Red Pentecostés Online ("RPO," "we," "us," or "our") is a mobile application providing Christian radio programming, Bible reading, and related church information. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Red Pentecostés Online mobile application. ## 1. Information We Collect Red Pentecostés Online does not require you to create an account, provide your name, email address, phone number, or password in order to use the app. We do not intentionally collect or sell personal information through the app. ## 2. Radio Streaming RPO connects to a third-party internet radio streaming service to provide live radio programming. When you listen to the radio, your device connects to the streaming service over the internet. The streaming provider may process technical information necessary to deliver the audio stream, such as network and device information, according to that provider's own policies. ## 3. Bible Content RPO provides Bible Scripture through API.Bible. When Scripture is requested, the app communicates with API.Bible to retrieve the requested Bible content. API.Bible may process technical information associated with requests according to its own privacy policies and terms. RPO does not create a user account for Bible reading. ## 4. Bible Highlights RPO allows users to highlight Bible verses. Highlight information is stored locally on the user's device using the app's local storage. These highlights are private to that device and are not currently synchronized to an online account or transferred automatically to another device. Highlights can remain available after closing and reopening the app and after restarting the device. If the app is uninstalled or its application data is cleared, locally stored highlight information may be deleted. Because the Bible text is currently retrieved through API.Bible, previously saved highlight information may remain on the device even when an internet connection is unavailable, but the associated Scripture text may not be available until the app can retrieve it again. ## 5. External Links and Services RPO may provide links or buttons that open third-party services, including: * WhatsApp * Facebook * Google Maps * The church's website * Other external websites or services When you choose to use one of these services, you may leave the RPO application and interact directly with the third-party service. Those services have their own privacy policies and terms. RPO does not control how third-party services collect or use information. ## 6. Data Storage Some app settings and user-created information, such as Bible highlight information, may be stored locally on your device. RPO does not currently provide cloud synchronization of Bible highlights. ## 7. Children's Privacy RPO is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. ## 8. Data Security We take reasonable measures to limit the collection of personal information through RPO. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, services, or data practices change. Any updated version will be made available at this webpage, and the effective date will be updated when appropriate. ## 10. Contact For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Red Pentecostés Online, please contact the RPO project administrator through the contact information provided in the application or its associated project materials. --- **Red Pentecostés Online** Privacy Policy Effective date: August 24, 2026
